# Research to Content

**ID:** `research-to-content`

**Outcome:** Usable content whose material factual claims can be traced to sources.

Use when a user requests Research to Content or a research synthesis plus content adaptation. Apply the [shared pack contract](../references/outcome-packs.md).

## Inputs

Required: readable source material or a topic with permission and tools to research it. Use the audience, desired channel, length, language, and selected profile when provided. A URL is not proof the page was read. Do not use a profile to override source evidence.

## Method

1. Read supplied sources; research current or unfamiliar material as needed with available tools. Record source IDs, titles, locations/URLs, publication dates when known, and actual access limits. Never fabricate a source or date.
2. Use U09 to map material claims to evidence, quotations to exact locations, and opinions/inferences to their authors. Note conflicting evidence, scope limits, and freshness.
3. Use U04 to synthesize what the evidence supports, including limitations. Differentiate the date an event occurred from the date an article reported it.
4. Use I02 to develop three distinct angles. Recommend one based on the actual audience, not a promise of virality. Do not change the strength of evidence to make a hook sharper.
5. Use C09 or the relevant channel workflow for one selected draft. Default to a concise general-purpose post when no channel is specified, not a guessed platform limit.
6. Trace every material claim in the draft back to the source map. Preserve uncertainty, avoid misleading excerpting, and follow applicable quotation/copyright limits. Research content should carry citations in the host's supported format.

## Default output contract

| Deliverable | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| `source-map` | Source IDs, actual locations, supported claims, dates/limits, and unresolved disagreements |
| `summary` | Key findings, what they do and do not establish, and relevant uncertainty |
| `angles` | Three distinct directions and one recommendation, adjustable to user count |
| `draft` | One copy-ready piece in the requested language/channel with supported claims and attribution |
| `verification-notes` | What was checked, what could not be checked, and claims deliberately excluded |

If a source is inaccessible, do not manufacture its summary. Work from readable material, identify the missing source, and block any claim dependent on it. Do not cite a page merely because it has a suitable title. For user-owned files, use available file citations or stable filenames/sections rather than invented public URLs.

## Example invocation

```text
Use Brief to Result: Research to Content.
Compare these two reports, suggest three angles, and draft one post
for product managers. Separate measured findings from interpretation.
```

**Done means:** the draft fulfills its hook, material claims have accessible support or explicit limitations, and unsupported claims are removed rather than disguised as facts.
